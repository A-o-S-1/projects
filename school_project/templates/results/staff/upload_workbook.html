Upload Class Result Workbook

Upload one class/arm's Excel workbook (REGISTER + term sheets + broadsheet) in a single step. New students in the REGISTER are added to the system automatically.

How This Works

Upload the school's standard result workbook exactly as produced — one file per class/arm:

  • REGISTER sheet — identifies the class (via its class code, e.g. J1A, S2B) and creates any student not already in the system.
  • 1ST TERM / 2ND TERM / 3RD TERM sheets — whichever are present get their scores written in. Upload again later once more terms are filled in — nothing is overwritten except the specific subject scores in that sheet.
  • BROADSHEET sheet — the finalized annual totals and promotion decision.

Positions and class averages are recalculated automatically after every upload.
